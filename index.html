Minh Nhat Vu

Minh is a Founding Member & and a Chief of Robotics at VinRobotics. He is also a Visiting Assistant Professor at VinUniversity (CAIR - Center for AI Research), and a Scientist at TU Wien (ACIN). His work spans real-time optimal control (MPC / trajectory optimization / motion planning) and robot learning (VLA/VLM for manipulation, grasping, and navigation), with an emphasis on deployable systems. He focuses on end-to-end integration, most recently improving humanoid locomotion through a fast closed-chain kinematics solver, low-latency middleware, real-time EtherCAT control, reinforcment learning for humanoid & manipulation ,and real-world industrial deployments.

What I'm working on

I am learning to build robots end-to-end: from the motors, EtherCAT buses, and real-time controllers all the way up to the learning systems (RL, imitation, and VLA models) that decide what to do next. Working side by side with my colleagues at VinRobotics, we try to turn research ideas into machines that do genuinely useful work in the real world, while still pushing the underlying science forward. These days I'm especially excited about three directions:

Humanoid loco-manipulation. Whole-body skills that use the entire humanoid body to get real work done: walking while carrying heavy loads, staying balanced under contact, and coordinating legs and arms for dynamic, expressive motion. Demos: heavy-payload support • autonomous carrying • human-style walking • RoboDance
Robots that learn from experience and reason over long tasks. Teaching robots directly from human demonstration and video, and giving them the memory to plan, track their own progress, and recover gracefully when something goes wrong across long, multi-step jobs. Demos: learning to dance from video • language-driven grasping • bin-picking & kitting • RL for manipulation
Dexterity and safe, dependable operation around people. Capable hands and controllers that are precise enough for industrial tasks yet safe and reliable when working right next to humans in messy, unstructured environments. Demos: 5F-D 12-DoF hand • real-time EtherCAT control • industrial deployment

If any of this resonates, whether you're a student, a researcher, or an industry partner, I'd love to hear from you. I'm always open to collaborate, so please reach out.
